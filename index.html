My Philosophy

I take a user-centered approach to AI engineering, building systems around real needs rather than technology for its own sake. Those users may be interacting with the product directly or be fellow engineers who depend on a clear, reliable developer experience. Across both, I continue to prioritize usability, accessibility, performance, and simplicity. I believe an AI system earns its place through measurable usefulness and transparency, giving people a clear understanding of what it does, how it helps, and where human judgment remains essential.

I design AI systems to improve how work happens, especially by automating repetitive tasks that distract people from higher-value decisions and creative problem-solving. My focus is on reusable capabilities, observable workflows, and thoughtful safeguards that allow teams to work and iterate confidently. The goal is not simply to add AI to an existing process, but to reconsider the process itself and create something more effective. Through collaboration and continuous feedback, I build systems that provide immediate value while remaining understandable, adaptable, and sustainable.

Upskilling

Over the past year, my growth has come primarily through applying new skills to real production challenges. I deepened my frontend expertise by leading migrations across React, Vite, Storybook, MUI, and Emotion while expanding my approach to automated testing and accessibility through Playwright and WCAG 2.2 AA work. Shipping these changes alongside major features and large-scale refactors reinforced an important lesson: the most valuable learning happens when new knowledge must hold up against real users, existing systems, and long-term maintainability.

My focus has since expanded from frontend development into AI engineering. I designed and now operate an agent-driven software delivery system built around specialized agents, reusable skills, MCP integrations, model routing, automated verification, and deliberate human oversight. That work changed how I think about engineering, from completing individual tasks to designing safe, observable systems that help people accomplish more. I have also shared what I learned through an internal AI Guild, an AI development insights channel, and a company-wide presentation so the benefits can extend beyond my own workflow.
